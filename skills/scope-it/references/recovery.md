# Publication recovery and Map maintenance

Read this for interrupted publication, uncertain creations, retrieval or amendment of an existing Map, legacy Map markers, or a delivery audit. Keep recovery records outside the formal Map; they do not supply missing source content, invocation consent or write authority.

## Resume an interrupted phase

Recover exact drafts, approvals, handoff inputs and successful writes. Read back actual content and identities before deciding what remains. Reuse complete approved work; resume the unfinished source phase or `to-afk-agent` handoff rather than rerunning completed sources.

Pass the prepared Map, permitted substitutions, original parent and known comment identity back to `to-afk-agent` for publication recovery. Its supplied-publication workflow owns lookup before retry, unique-document recovery and readback. Retain the returned canonical ID/URL; ambiguous or conflicting matches remain unresolved.

Preserve successful writes, siblings and unrelated content. Relay existing Carry, source/worktree disposition and removal evidence independently of Map publication; one completed operation does not complete the handoff. Preserve unanswered legacy source choices. Verify remaining writes and both relationship axes before dependent work. A missing draft needs its complete source content and authority restored.

## Retrieve or amend the Map

For a later read-only retrieval, return the parent and published canonical Map without starting a new handoff or readiness writes. Its embedded **Start the Next Ticket** protocol lets another session continue without this skill or the planning chat.

Prepare approved amendments for the same canonical comment and delegate their publication to `to-afk-agent`. Supply the marker-bound editing boundary and preserve surrounding content and existing Scope/Ticket bytes. Later ordinary comments do not change the Map identity. Preserve legacy `scope-it-remake` markers and their comment binding. The handoff workflow coordinates active execution or automatic pickup before changing executor inputs.

Return source-content changes to their responsible source for its required checks and publication approval before affected downstream work. Preserve unchanged artifacts and approvals. Changes to ownership, delivery paths or shared responsibilities need an approved Map amendment.

## Audit or repair drift

A requested delivery audit reads the published agreement and repository workflow; corrective writes require approval. Report mismatched content, metadata, attachments, relationships, canonical Map identity or readiness evidence, and withhold affected completion until resolved. Route approved source corrections to their source and handoff corrections to `to-afk-agent`. Preserve completed work while obtaining only missing repair decisions. Failed readback of a supported capability remains unresolved rather than becoming an unsupported fallback.
