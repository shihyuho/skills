# Publication recovery and Map maintenance

Read this for interrupted publication, uncertain creations, retrieval of a published plan, changes to published Tickets or blockers, amendment of an existing Map, legacy Map markers, or a delivery audit. Keep recovery records outside the Map; they do not supply missing source content, invocation consent or write authority.

## Resume an interrupted phase

Recover exact drafts, approvals, handoff inputs and successful writes. Read back actual content and identities before deciding what remains. Reuse complete approved work; resume the unfinished source phase, Ticket Map publication or `to-afk-agent` handoff rather than rerunning completed work.

Before retrying a Ticket Map publication, read all of the parent's comments for its markers, including legacy ones, and its approved content. Bind a unique complete match by its read-back ID/URL. Several candidates, or a lookup that could not read every comment, leave the identity unresolved; create a comment only after a complete lookup finds none. Recency and similar fragments do not choose the Map's comment.

Preserve successful writes, siblings and unrelated content. Relay existing Carry, source/worktree disposition and removal evidence to the handoff; one completed operation does not complete the handoff. Preserve unanswered legacy source choices. Verify remaining writes and both relationship axes before dependent work. A missing draft needs its complete source content and authority restored.

## Retrieve or amend the Map

For a later read-only retrieval, return the parent and its Ticket Map without starting a new handoff or readiness writes; with one Ticket, return the parent and its Ticket comment. The parent's native relations let another session continue without this skill or the planning chat.

When Tickets or their native blockers change, redraw the Map for the same comment and present the proposed change as a `diff` of the drawn view when approval is needed. Preserve surrounding content and existing Scope/Ticket bytes; later ordinary comments do not change the Map identity. The Map is not an executor input, so redrawing it needs no coordination with dispatched runs; Ticket and relationship changes keep their own source and handoff paths.

Legacy `scope-it:delivery-map` and `scope-it-remake` markers identify earlier Maps; treat them as the parent's Map when looking it up, and leave the comment unchanged until an approved amendment. That amendment rewrites the same comment in the Ticket Map format between `scope-it:ticket-map` markers, keeping any Planning Carry section verbatim until a Carry record from `to-afk-agent` covers it.

Return source-content changes to their responsible source for its required checks and publication approval before affected downstream work. Preserve unchanged artifacts and approvals. Landing-obligation changes need approval here; delivery-path changes belong to `to-afk-agent`.

## Audit or repair drift

A requested delivery audit reads the published agreement and repository workflow; corrective writes require approval. Report mismatched content, metadata, attachments, relationships, Ticket Map identity or readiness evidence, and withhold affected completion until resolved. Route approved source corrections to their source and handoff corrections to `to-afk-agent`. Preserve completed work while obtaining only missing repair decisions. Failed readback of a supported capability remains unresolved rather than becoming an unsupported fallback.
